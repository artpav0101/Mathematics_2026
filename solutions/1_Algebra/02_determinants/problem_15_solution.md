### Exercise 15. Combining Determinant Properties

Given invertible $2\times2$ matrices $A$ and $B$, with $\det A=-2$ and $\det B=3$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(B^{-1}A^TB)
&=\det(B^{-1})\det(A^T)\det(B) \\
&=\frac{1}{\det B}\det A\det B \\
&=\frac{1}{3}(-2)(3) \\
&=-2.
\end{aligned}
$$

The properties used, in order, are the determinant-of-a-product rule, $\det(B^{-1})=1/\det B$, and $\det(A^T)=\det A$. The factors $\det(B^{-1})=1/3$ and $\det B=3$ cancel, leaving $\det A=-2$.

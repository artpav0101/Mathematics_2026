### Exercise 9. Area and the Determinant

Place the vectors as columns of a matrix:

$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 1 \\
1 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then

$$
\det M=3\cdot4-1\cdot1=11.
$$

The parallelogram's area is the absolute value of the determinant, so its area is $|\det M|=11$ square units.

Reversing the vectors gives

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
=1\cdot1-3\cdot4=-11.
$$

The determinant changes sign when the columns are swapped, but the area remains the absolute value, $11$ square units.

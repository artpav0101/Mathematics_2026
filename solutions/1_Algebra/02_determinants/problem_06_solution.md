### Exercise 6. Parameter and Invertibility

For

$$
A(t)=
\begin{pmatrix}
t & 1 \\
2 & t \\
\end{pmatrix},
$$

the determinant is

$$
\det A(t)=t^2-2.
$$

The matrix is not invertible exactly when its determinant is zero:

$$
t^2-2=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
t=\pm\sqrt{2}.
$$

Therefore, the matrix is not invertible for $t=\sqrt{2}$ or $t=-\sqrt{2}$.

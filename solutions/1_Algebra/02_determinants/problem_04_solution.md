### Exercise 4. Triangular Matrix

The determinant of a triangular matrix is the product of its diagonal entries. Hence,

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 & 1 \\
0 & -3 & 5 \\
0 & 0 & 7 \\
\end{pmatrix}
=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7=-42.
$$

In general, for any upper- or lower-triangular matrix, the determinant equals the product of the diagonal entries.
